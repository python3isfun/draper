# Examples: drama reference images

From the sample drama "The Last Month's Rent" (Maya Collins's used bookstore in a small Ohio
river town; her father Frank; the old family photo). Each follows h3_image_rules.md: what the
image is first, layout, visible style, light by its direction, exact counts, quoted text or
the no-text sentence, and for edits the definitions → target → relationships → changes
paragraph.

## Character identity: text-to-image, 3552x4736 (3:4)

Brief: Frank Collins, about sixty, hollow cheeks, deep lines, short unkempt gray hair, eyes that
avoid yours; a faded brown canvas work jacket. Must keep: the old burn scar on the back of his
right hand.

```text
A studio casting photograph of Frank Collins, a man of about sixty, framed as a close
head-and-shoulders portrait with his right hand raised beside his collar so the back of the hand
faces the camera. He is front-facing with his eyes turned slightly away from the lens. His face
is lean with hollow cheeks and deep lines across the forehead and around the eyes; his short gray
hair is a little unkempt. An old, pale, irregular burn scar the size of a quarter covers the back
of his right hand between the thumb and index-finger knuckles. He wears a faded brown canvas work
jacket with softened seams over a gray T-shirt. A plain light gray seamless backdrop sits behind
him. Soft light from the front left models his face, with gentle fill from the right and natural
skin texture. The image contains no text, letters or numbers.
```

## Prop with a picture inside: text-to-image, 4096x4096 (1:1)

```text
A product photograph of an old framed family photo lying on a neutral gray countertop, seen
nearly straight on, the palm-sized wooden frame filling most of the image with all four worn
corners visible. The faded color print shows exactly three people, fifteen years younger than
today: the father on the left, a man in his forties with short dark hair and a pale button-down

scar on it; the mother in the middle, in a plain blouse; and Maya on the right, a girl of about

from the upper left with gentle fill from the right. The image contains no text, letters or
```
